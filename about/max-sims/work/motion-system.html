Max Sims/Motion Design

Motion System

2024MotionIdentityAfter Effects

Overview

Animated brand identity package for a fintech startup — logo reveals, transitions, and social templates.

A static brand identity is only part of the picture now. When Meridian came to us with a new visual identity already in place, they needed the motion layer — how the logo enters, how the brand moves between states, what the UI feels like in motion.

The motion system started with a single principle: nothing snaps. Every transition has a physical quality — weight, inertia, ease-out. The logo reveal was built around a single arc motion that echoes the brand's mark.

Deliverables: logo reveal in three durations (3s, 6s, 15s), transition toolkit in After Effects, social template suite in 6 formats, and a motion guidelines document that gave their internal team enough to extend the system independently.
