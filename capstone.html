Built for minds that work differently.

Most productivity tools are built for neurotypical attention. Streaks. Badges. Notifications. Red numbers on icons. They're engineered to keep you engaged — which is precisely the opposite of what ADHD brains need.

Adaptive Clarity starts from a different premise: the most useful tool is the one that gets out of your way. No gamification. No data harvesting. No silent background monitoring. Just calm, ambient support — on your terms.

The result is a dual-component system: a mobile app for voice-first session management and an ambient physical companion that communicates through light, not noise.

The Solution

Two components. One system.

Mobile App

Mobile App

Voice-first focus session management powered by the Claude API. Speak a task, get grounded — no typing, no menus, no friction.

  • Voice-initiated focus sessions — speak to start, not tap through menus
  • AI-assisted context prompts, triggered by you — never automatic
  • Session summaries without judgment or streaks
  • No notifications, no badge counts, no red numbers
  • Firebase-backed, minimal data collection, full transparency

Physical Companion

Physical Companion

A desk-sitting ambient light device that communicates focus state through peripheral vision — not urgent alerts.

  • Circular ambient display registers in peripheral vision, not direct attention
  • Organic form factor — fabric base, smooth polymer head, approachable presence
  • LED states: warm white (focus), amber (advisory), soft green (break), dim blue (idle)
  • No sound, no vibration, no pop-ups — ever
  • Bluetooth LE connection to the app

Light States

Focus

Slow steady pulse, 2.5s cycle

Break

Gentle fade, 3s cycle

Advisory

Slow pulse — "notice this", not alarm

Idle

Static, 30% brightness

How It Works

Quiet by design.

01

Pair the companion

Connect the physical device to the app via Bluetooth. It takes thirty seconds and stays paired.

02

Start a session

Open the app and speak your task. The AI confirms and sets the focus state. The companion responds with ambient light.

03

Let it do its job

Work. The system communicates through light in your peripheral vision — never interrupting, never demanding attention.

User Research — Open Now

Help shape Adaptive Clarity.

User testing is open through May 2026. Participants help define how the system actually works for real ADHD minds — your feedback directly shapes the final product.

Who We're Looking For

  • Adults with ADHD (formal diagnosis or strong self-identification)
  • College students or knowledge workers who struggle with standard productivity apps
  • People who find streaks, notifications, or gamification anxiety-inducing
  • Anyone curious about calm technology and ethical AI design

What to Expect

  • A 45–60 minute session: brief onboarding, structured focus task, debrief
  • Use both the mobile app and physical prototype together
  • Short follow-up survey (under 10 minutes)
  • No personal data stored beyond session notes — full transparency
